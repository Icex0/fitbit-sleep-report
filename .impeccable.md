# Design Context

**Audience:** the person who owns the Fitbit, reviewing their own sleep at a desk, and possibly
their GP or a sleep specialist looking over their shoulder or at a printout.

**Use cases:**
- See the long-term picture: sleep timing and regularity, duration, oxygen, heart rate, breathing.
- Open one night and read it like a polysomnography summary: stages, SpO2, heart rate, events.
- Read the literature report: which published findings apply to their numbers, with citations.

**Tone:** professional and clinical, calm, trustworthy. Like a printed sleep-study report from a
good sleep lab, not a fitness app. No gamification, no "scores", no alarmist colour.

**Direction: "Sleep lab report".** Warm paper background, serif headings, ink-blue data, hairline
rules, small-caps section labels, tabular numerals, generous margins. Light by default with a
designed dark mode (ink on dark paper, not neon). Prints well.

**Avoid:** component-kit look (shadcn etc.), card grids, glassmorphism, gradients, glowing dark
dashboards, hero-metric templates, emoji, icon-above-every-heading.
